06 / RULEBOOK

Play hard.
Play fair.

The championship is competitive, but the experience stays student-first. All registered players must follow the rules before, during, and after their matches.

Competition rules

  1. Every team must submit its final roster before the registration deadline.
  2. Players must use the registered account and team identity during matches.
  3. A match result must be reported by the captain after the game ends.
  4. Unsporting behaviour may be referred to the event organisers.

Quick checklist

  • Stable internet connection
  • Correct game version installed
  • Team captain present before check-in
  • Ready 15 minutes before match time

08 / ABOUT ME

Meet the creator of Campus Rivals.

Hi, I’m Shaurya Sindhu, a student at Scaler School of Technology and the creator of Campus Rivals.

I created this project to combine my interest in technology, gaming, and student communities into a single college esports experience.

The website demonstrates Term 1 concepts including semantic HTML, lists, tables, forms, the box model, Flexbox, Grid, media queries, positioning, and CSS animations.
